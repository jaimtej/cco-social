# Down Payment Assistance for First-Time Buyers in Jacksonville and Duval County

One of the biggest myths we hear from first-time buyers is "We need 20% down." For a lot of Jacksonville families, that simply isn't true.

Florida and the City of Jacksonville both run programs that help with the down payment and closing costs. They aren't magic, and they come with rules. But for the right buyer, they can be the difference between renting another year and getting the keys. Here's how the main programs work as of November 2026.

## First, how down payment assistance works

Most assistance is not a grant. It's usually a **second mortgage** that sits behind your main loan. The difference is in how it gets paid back:

- **Deferred:** no monthly payment, but the full amount is due when you sell, refinance, pay off the first mortgage or move out.
- **Forgivable:** the balance shrinks each year you stay, until it's gone.
- **Amortizing:** a small monthly payment, like any other loan.

You also have to use a participating lender. Florida Housing's assistance is only available with a Florida Housing first mortgage. It isn't offered as stand-alone help.

## Florida Housing programs

Florida Housing Finance Corporation offers 30-year fixed-rate first mortgages for first-time buyers through approved lenders, paired with these assistance options:

| Program | Amount | How it's repaid |
|---|---|---|
| FL Assist | Up to $10,000 | 0%, deferred, not forgiven. Due in full when you sell, refinance, pay off the first mortgage or stop living there |
| HFA Preferred PLUS / HFA Advantage PLUS | 3%, 4% or 5% of the loan amount | Forgiven 20% per year over 5 years. Conventional loans only |
| FL HLP | $12,500 | 3% second mortgage with monthly payments over 30 years |

FL Assist works with FHA, VA, USDA and conventional loans. The PLUS option is often the most attractive because it can disappear entirely if you stay five years, but it only pairs with Florida Housing's conventional HFA Preferred or HFA Advantage loans. Your lender can tell you which one fits.

## Florida Hometown Heroes

Hometown Heroes is aimed at Florida's workforce: teachers, healthcare workers, law enforcement, firefighters, military members, veterans, childcare workers, correctional officers, court employees and other qualifying roles. Buyers generally need to work full time for a Florida-based employer and meet county income limits.

- **Amount:** 5% of the first mortgage, from $10,000 up to $35,000
- **Terms:** 0% interest, deferred, no monthly payment. Repaid when you sell, refinance, transfer the home or stop living in it.

**Funding status:** This program runs in rounds. Florida Housing reported that the 2025 round committed all of its funding for the 2025-2026 fiscal year in about six months. The state's 2026-27 budget added $50 million, and the program reopened July 13, 2026, first come, first served until funds are fully committed. We couldn't confirm from an official source how much remains today, so ask a participating lender before you count on it.

## City of Jacksonville: Headstart to Homeownership (H2H)

The City's H2H program offers up to $25,000 toward down payment and closing costs for buyers in Duval County. Here's what the City's most recent program flyer (April 2025) lists:

- **Income:** at or below 80% of area median income. That was $57,400 for a one-person household and $82,000 for a family of four, effective April 1, 2025. HUD updates these limits every year.
- **Price cap:** $304,000 maximum purchase price or value.
- **Your money in:** a minimum $500 down payment plus the cost of a home inspection.
- **Education:** an eight-hour homeownership class with an approved provider.
- **The home:** single-family, townhome or condo in Jacksonville (no mobile or manufactured homes), and it must pass a housing quality inspection before closing.
- **Terms:** 0% interest for 10 years, with the balance reducing each year. Sell or move out early and the remaining balance comes due.
- **Order matters:** get approved through a participating lender before you sign a sales contract.

The City's Housing Services page lists H2H as subject to funding limitations. Over the last five years, the program has awarded assistance on 396 homes, and Jacksonville Today reported that the City's buyer assistance programs tend to close quickly each year because demand is high.

## What most programs require

Every program has its own rules, but these come up again and again:

- **Credit score:** Florida Housing's loans require a minimum 640. Lenders may set their own bar.
- **First-time buyer:** for Florida Housing, generally no ownership of your primary residence in the past three years. H2H requires that you not currently own a home.
- **Homebuyer education:** an approved course is required.
- **Income and price limits:** set by county and updated regularly.
- **Primary residence:** you have to live in the home.

## Terms change. Confirm before you plan around them.

We want to be clear: funding, amounts and limits on these programs change, sometimes mid-year. What we've shared here is accurate to the official pages as of early November 2026, but it isn't financial advice for your situation. Before you make an offer, sit down with a participating lender and confirm what's open, what you qualify for, and whether programs can be combined.

## Don't stop at the program

Assistance helps with the cash to close. The monthly payment is a separate puzzle. On new construction, builders often offer incentives like rate buydowns. On resale homes, sellers can sometimes help with closing costs. We can show you how those incentives would change your payment.

## The bottom line

You may not need 20% down. Florida Housing's programs, Hometown Heroes and the City's H2H program can all help first-time buyers in Duval County, but each one is a loan with its own payback terms, limits and funding window. Start early, find a participating lender, take the homebuyer class, and have your paperwork ready so you can move when the money is there.
