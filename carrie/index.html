Recommended music

Any song you like works. A few pointers that fit our look:

  • Keep it calm and polished. Soft piano, acoustic guitar or mellow instrumentals match the brand better than loud trending songs.
  • Seller and market stories (like Week One Offer): calm and confident, nothing too upbeat.
  • Listings and neighborhoods (like Acosta): warm and acoustic.
  • Instrumental beats lyrics when there's text on screen, so people can read it.
  • Keep the volume low enough that it doesn't feel like an ad.
